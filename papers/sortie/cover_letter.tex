\opening{Dear Editor,}

I submit the manuscript \emph{SORTIE: Fair Vehicle Routing When Route
Durations Are Random} for consideration as an article in
\emph{Transportation Science}.

Recent exact work on fair vehicle routing combines ordered fairness measures
(range, Gini mean difference, mean absolute deviation) with a bilevel
requirement that every route be a shortest tour of its customers. That work
assumes deterministic travel times. Drivers experience realised durations,
and the fairness they perceive is the fairness of the realised day. The
manuscript studies this setting and makes four contributions.

\begin{enumerate}
\item It separates ex-ante from ex-post fairness and bounds the gap between
  them, the \emph{fairness illusion}, in terms of the routes' quadratic
  lengths. It also proves that multiplicative common shocks, such as weather
  or a fleet-wide congestion factor, leave expected ordered fairness
  unchanged.
\item It gives an exact, sampling-free formula for the expected value of any
  ordered measure of independent route durations, including the negative
  weights of dispersion measures. The formula is a Poisson--binomial
  order-statistic integral. Its derivatives, the \emph{rank prices}, show
  which routes a fairness objective would like to lengthen.
\item It shows that, under uncertainty, even the min--max objective can
  prefer a tour that is not the shortest. Route consistency must therefore be
  imposed explicitly. It is defined through each driver's own
  mean--standard-deviation criterion. Such tours are supported Pareto tours
  and can be computed by parametric Held--Karp.
\item It proposes SORTIE, a bilevel heuristic that screens moves with the
  rank prices. SORTIE is evaluated on instances derived from the CVRPLIB
  benchmark used by the deterministic literature, against deterministic and
  sample-average baselines, under both homogeneous and congestion-driven
  heterogeneous noise.
\end{enumerate}

Code, instances, solved plans and raw results will be made publicly
available.

The manuscript is original, has not been published, and is not under
consideration elsewhere. I have no conflicts of interest to declare.

In the 47 route--level rows where an estimator expects fewer than one draw in the weaker mode ($Np_{\mathcal B}<1$), its underestimate follows $s$: the Spearman correlation is $0.92$ and the median ratio of underestimate to $s$ is $1.00$. The single shift at $10^{-4}$ expects about $14$ such draws in median; it then reaches the weaker mode, and the deficit shows partly as error and partly as variance (relative variance $212$ in Table~\ref{tab:rivals}). On the routes with $\varrho\le0.75$, $s<10^{-15}$ and both estimators are unbiased within their sampling error.

\noindent Dear Editor,

Please find enclosed the second revision of our manuscript
CAOR-D-26-01885, \emph{``Optimal-stopping recourse for vehicle routing
with simultaneous pickup, delivery, and stochastic demands.''} We thank
you and the reviewers for the second round of reports. Reviewer~1
considers the principal concerns resolved; Reviewer~2 asked us to align
several methodological claims and implementation descriptions with the
evidence, and Reviewer~3 asked four clarifying questions. Every comment
is answered in the enclosed response letter, and a version of the
manuscript with the changes marked against revision~1 is provided.

The main changes are the following.

\begin{itemize}
\item The state-conditional variant of the policy is now described as an
  approximation, with the information it retains and a condition for the
  monotonicity of its fresh-start value, checked empirically.
\item The discussion of the fresh-start bias defines its estimands,
  reports bootstrap intervals and limits its conclusions to what the
  evidence supports; the reference programs are labelled separately.
\item Two conventions in the evaluation code were corrected (fully loaded
  departures keep zero slack; the physical post-return state is capped at
  an empty vehicle) and the affected experiments were re-run. No
  conclusion changed.
\item The day-type experiment, the statistical wording, the
  over-triggering bound and the exact benchmark (now with a
  grid-refinement check) are stated more precisely, and the answers to
  Reviewer~3's questions are reflected in the text and in one table.
\item The repository states of the submitted versions are tagged, so the
  code can be checked against each version under review.
\end{itemize}

The revised manuscript has not been published previously and is not
under consideration elsewhere. The authors have no conflicts of interest
to declare.

Thank you for your consideration.

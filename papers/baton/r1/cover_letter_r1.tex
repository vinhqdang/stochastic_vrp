\noindent Dear Editor,

Please find enclosed the first revision of our manuscript CAOR-D-26-01885,
\emph{``Optimal-stopping recourse for vehicle routing with simultaneous
pickup, delivery, and stochastic demands.''} We thank you and the three
reviewers for constructive reports. Every comment is answered point by
point in the enclosed response letter, and a version of the manuscript
with all changes marked is provided.

The main changes are the following.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Theory.} The assumptions are weakened: the load need only
  be a stochastically monotone Markov chain, and no ordering between
  handoff and emergency prices is required. A new proposition bounds the
  cost of over-triggering by the myopic threshold. We also corrected two
  errors of our own in the original propositions.
\item \textbf{Machine-checked proofs.} All four propositions are now
  verified formally in the Lean~4 proof assistant with the Mathlib
  library. The formalisation compiles without unproved steps, relies only
  on Lean's standard axioms, and is released with the replication code;
  the manuscript lists its modelling abstractions. The verification also
  exposed a side condition of the first proposition, now stated.
\item \textbf{Stronger benchmarks.} New competitors that have the same
  two levers as our policy (a two-lever threshold rule and an equal-data
  three-action dynamic program), a position-dependent threshold, a
  three-action clairvoyant bound, and an exact dynamic program for
  independent demand, against which our policy attains \exDetShare{} of
  the optimal saving on deterministic plans.
\item \textbf{Robustness.} Dependent demand, non-exchangeable days, a
  capacitated standby pool shared across routes, training-data budgets,
  single-core timing, deliver-only customers and a depot-return fee.
\item \textbf{Statistics and reporting.} The instance is the experimental
  unit, with paired bootstrap intervals and Holm-adjusted tests; ties and
  the one setting in which a competitor is ahead are reported as such,
  and several claims of the original version are narrowed accordingly.
\end{itemize}

Supplementary tables, one figure and one proof have moved to an
appendix. All tables and figures regenerate from the public repository,
which also contains the formal proofs.

The revised manuscript has not been published previously and is not
under consideration elsewhere. The authors have no conflicts of interest
to declare.

Thank you for your consideration.

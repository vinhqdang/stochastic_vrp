## 3. Ranked shortlist of baselines to implement

1. **Zhu-Adulyasak-Rousseau 2026 (Networks), ALP value function with outsourcing recourse.** Closest published problem: stochastic demand, recourse by handing demand to an external carrier, value function by approximate LP.
   Sketch: choose basis functions phi(k, W, remaining-demand stats) (affine in W and in remaining mean/var, per-stop intercepts, as in their affine lower bounds); solve the ALP min c'theta s.t. phi(s)'theta <= cost(s,a) + E[phi(s')'theta] on states *sampled* from our simulator (constraint sampling); expectations by sample average over the per-stop demand distributions. Policy: hand off iff handoff cost <= stage cost + E[V(s')]. Compare to our regression-MC fit on the same features: this isolates "LP fit vs regression fit" of the same continuation value.

2. **Yang-Mathur-Ballou threshold DP, as re-established by Venkateshan-Mathur 2026 (NRL).** Gives the published structural baseline for the scalar state.
   Sketch: on each fixed route, backward DP over (k, W) with the per-stop demand pmf, which yields a per-stop load threshold W_k* (handoff iff W outside the continue region). This is our exact (k, W) DP; citing NRL 2026 shows the threshold structure is proven, and the isotonic fit should recover W_k*. Report threshold agreement as a sanity check.

3. **DQN with set/graph-attention encoder (Dastpak-Errico-Jabali, C&OR 2023; arXiv 2026).** A TD-learning counterpart to our regression-MC learner on the same rich state.
   Sketch: Q(s, a) for a in {continue, handoff} with our set-encoder transformer as the GAT stand-in; double Q, replay 50k, minibatch 32, epsilon decay as in their Sec. 5 settings; train on the same simulated paths. Compare recourse cost and training cost at matched simulation budget. Their repo (github.com/moda707/vrp-sdo) provides instance generators to check settings; cite the 2023 C&OR paper as the peer-reviewed source.

4. **PPO policy with GNN encoder (Kadyrov et al. 2025, OR Perspectives).** Direct policy search instead of a value function.
   Sketch: Bernoulli policy pi(handoff | s) from the set encoder, PPO with action masking (no handoff at last stop), reward = negative stage cost; same training paths. Tests whether policy gradients reach the regressed-continuation-value result; expect higher variance.

5. **Randomised-NN LSM (Herrera et al. 2024) and deep optimal stopping (Becker et al. 2019).** Cheap extra regressors inside our existing LSM loop; RLSM is one least-squares solve per stage, so it costs almost nothing to add. Strengthens the "architecture does not matter" result with a published stopping-specific architecture.
